Delete your Suhbah data

Open Suhbah → Settings → Privacy → Delete all local data. Read the confirmation and choose Delete permanently.

This clears settings, bookmarks, notes, worship records and saved voice samples, disables feed background refresh and cancels local reminders. You can also clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall Suhbah.

No account is created and the developer cannot access or remotely erase your local records. Copies shared through an export and external provider logs are not removed. Email support for help or to request deletion of support correspondence.
