Privacy policy

Who is responsible

Momin (Android package app.sakina.android) is provided by Muhammad Kashif. For privacy questions, deletion assistance, content corrections or copyright concerns, email kashifash509@gmail.com. This policy describes the free Android release and the public policy website.

Information kept on your device

Settings, selected city or optional foreground location, prayer preferences, Quran bookmarks and notes, memorization progress, worship and fasting records, calculation inputs, saved mosque times, followed sources and recent-tool history are stored locally. Momin has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics service or developer-operated cloud sync. We do not sell your personal data. Local storage is protected by the operating system sandbox; it is not an encrypted vault. Anyone with access to an unlocked device or an exported copy may be able to read it.

Optional location and notifications

You can choose a city manually without location permission. If you request device location or enable the compass, foreground location and sensors help calculate prayer times and Qibla. Momin does not request background location. Nearby-mosque search is a separate optional online action: its Overpass provider receives the query coordinates, search radius and your IP address. Prayer reminders are scheduled locally with your permission; they do not use a remote push account. Revoke permissions in Android Settings at any time.

Microphone and voice training

Voice counting starts only when you activate it and grant microphone permission. This release requires on-device speech recognition and does not fall back to a network recognizer. Availability depends on your phone and installed language model. Your operating-system speech service may need a separate model download. Normal counting does not save audio. Voice training creates a temporary clip and saves only samples you explicitly confirm, up to three per phrase and language, in the local database. Delete samples in Train my voice or delete all local data. Momin does not upload these recordings.

Optional online services

Refreshing Hidayat sends requests to the selected publishers, including YouTube and Darul Ifta Birmingham. If you turn on daily background refresh, those requests can also occur when the app is closed; turn it off in Hidayat. Online calendar comparison sends selected dates to AlAdhan and MABIMS.dev, without GPS coordinates. Each service receives your IP address and ordinary connection/request metadata and may retain server logs under its own policy. Links open external websites or apps, whose practices are outside our control. Quran reading and prayer calculations work without these services. Quran audio streaming and purchases are disabled in this release.

Export, retention and deletion

Records remain until you remove them, clear app storage, uninstall the app, or use Privacy > Delete all local data. That action clears local records and voice samples, stops feed background refresh and cancels reminders. An export is a readable JSON copy, not a restorable backup; it excludes voice recordings. Nothing is sent to the developer by exporting. If you choose a recipient in the share sheet, that recipient gets the copy. Deleting the app cannot delete copies already shared, external provider logs, or support emails.

Support and privacy requests

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. Do not send worship logs, recordings or sensitive financial information unless necessary. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to resolve the request, meet applicable legal obligations and maintain a record of the resolution; ask for deletion at the contact above. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correction, erasure, objection or complaint to a data-protection authority. We do not have remote access to your on-device records.

Children, website and changes

Momin is a general-audience worship and study tool, not a service specifically directed at children. Parents should supervise microphone use, exports and external links. The public policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives normal web request data; it has no analytics added by Momin. See https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement for hosting practices. This policy will be updated when app behavior changes, with a new date shown here. Material changes requiring consent will be presented before the affected use.
