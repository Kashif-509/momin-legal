Privacy policy

Who is responsible

Suhbah (Android package app.sakina.android) is provided by Muhammad Kashif. For privacy questions, deletion assistance, content corrections or copyright concerns, email kashifash509@gmail.com. This policy describes the Android internal-test release with optional Premium subscriptions and the public policy website.

Information kept on your device

Settings, selected city or optional foreground location, prayer preferences, Quran bookmarks and notes, memorization progress, worship and fasting records, calculation inputs, saved mosque times, followed sources and recent-tool history are stored locally. No account is needed for local use. Optional cloud backup uses a Google account when the service is configured. Suhbah has no advertising SDK or analytics service. We do not sell your personal data. Local storage is protected by the operating system sandbox; it is not an encrypted vault. Anyone with access to an unlocked device or an exported copy may be able to read it.

Optional location and notifications

You can choose a city manually without location permission. If you request device location or enable the compass, foreground location and sensors help calculate prayer times and Qibla. Suhbah does not request background location. Nearby-mosque search is a separate optional online action: its Overpass provider receives the query coordinates, search radius and your IP address. Prayer reminders are scheduled locally with your permission; they do not use a remote push account. Revoke permissions in Android Settings at any time.

Microphone and voice training

Voice counting starts only when you activate it and grant microphone permission. On-device speech recognition is the default. Provider mode is a separate, optional action with a confirmation: your phone’s speech provider may send microphone audio to its servers and process it under its own privacy policy. This choice lasts only for the current screen session; there is no automatic network fallback. Voice training continues to require on-device recognition. Availability depends on your phone and installed language model. Your operating-system speech service may need a separate model download. Normal counting does not save audio. Voice training creates a temporary clip and saves only samples you explicitly confirm, up to three per phrase and language, in the local database. Delete samples in Train my voice or delete all local data. Suhbah does not upload these recordings.

Private voice lessons

Learn together saves parent or teacher recordings, learner attempts, display names, timed corrections and human review markers on this device. Recording starts only after you grant permission and tap record, with a maximum of 90 seconds per clip. Record other people only with their permission; parents or guardians should supervise recordings involving children. No voice cloning, voice identification or automated tajweed grading is performed. You can explicitly share a lesson file containing names, recordings and feedback through another app. Its recipient can import, retain and forward that copy; this is not an encrypted messaging service. Sharing through another app does not send a copy to the developer; optional cloud backup is described below. Imported files are merged locally. Delete a lesson in Learn together or use Delete all local data. Cloud backups exclude voice lessons and audio. Use lesson-file export to keep a separate copy; there is no automatic teacher delivery.

Optional online services

Refreshing Hidayat sends requests to the selected publishers, including YouTube and Darul Ifta Birmingham. If you turn on daily background refresh, those requests can also occur when the app is closed; turn it off in Hidayat. Online calendar comparison sends selected dates to AlAdhan and MABIMS.dev, without GPS coordinates. Each service receives your IP address and ordinary connection/request metadata and may retain server logs under its own policy. Links open external websites or apps, whose practices are outside our control. Quran reading and prayer calculations work without these services. Quran audio streaming remains disabled. Optional Premium purchases use Google Play Billing: Google processes payments and purchase/account information under its policies. Suhbah queries purchase status and product prices through Google Play and does not receive card details or upload worship records. This internal test uses device-side purchase checks; it has no developer billing server. Cattle estimates fetch public listing pages from Punjab e-Cattle Market using a coarse market division inferred from your selected city, without GPS coordinates. Hajj and Umrah estimates fetch public package pages from the named travel providers. Opening these Premium estimate screens refreshes saved results after two calendar months; Refresh checks again immediately. Providers receive your IP address and request metadata. Asking prices are planning estimates, not confirmed offers or endorsements.

Export, retention and deletion

Records remain until you remove them, clear app storage, uninstall the app, or use Privacy > Delete all local data. That action clears local records and voice samples, stops feed background refresh and cancels reminders. An export is a readable JSON copy, not a restorable backup; it excludes voice recordings. Nothing is sent to the developer by exporting. If you choose a recipient in the share sheet, that recipient gets the copy. Deleting the app cannot delete copies already shared, external provider logs, or support emails. Learn together has a separate, restorable lesson-file export that includes its audio and feedback. The general privacy export includes lesson metadata without audio. Deletion also removes voice lessons and their recordings. Delete all local data signs out and disables automatic backup but does not delete existing cloud backups. Use Account & backup > Delete cloud account & backups to delete those separately.

Support and privacy requests

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. Do not send worship logs, recordings or sensitive financial information unless necessary. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to resolve the request, meet applicable legal obligations and maintain a record of the resolution; ask for deletion at the contact above. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correction, erasure, objection or complaint to a data-protection authority. We do not have remote access to your on-device records.

Children, website and changes

Family voice lessons may be used with children under parent or guardian supervision. Adults should manage sharing, permissions, purchases and external links. Only use a nickname if a full name is unnecessary. The app has no child accounts, public profiles, voice cloning or developer-operated recording upload service. The public policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages and receives ordinary web request data; it has no analytics added by Suhbah. See https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement for hosting practices. This policy is updated when app behaviour changes.

Optional account and cloud backups

When cloud backup is available, Google sign-in shares your basic Google profile and email address with Supabase Auth to identify your account. Google handles your password; Suhbah does not receive it. If you choose Back up now or enable weekly backups, your local worship and fasting records, Quran notes and bookmarks, memorization progress, planner records including zakat calculations, settings including selected location, followed sources, are uploaded to developer-operated Supabase storage associated with your account. Voice lessons, recordings, voice-tasbeeh training samples and downloaded content are excluded. Only your authenticated account may read its backups through the app. Data is transferred over HTTPS and stored by Supabase; this is not end-to-end encryption and authorized service operators can access stored data. Only the latest successful snapshot is retained, up to 20 KiB of compressed records, until you delete your cloud account. Supabase may retain operational logs and infrastructure backups under its own retention policy. Weekly timing is best-effort and can be delayed by offline status, battery restrictions or force-stopping. Signing out stops uploads, but does not delete existing backups. Reinstall and sign in with the same Google account to restore; restoring replaces local records. You may delete your cloud account and backups in the app or request deletion assistance at kashifash509@gmail.com. Cloud backup is optional and no records are uploaded merely by signing in. Uploads and weekly backup require Premium; login and restoring an existing backup remain free. Subscription receipts are verified with Google Play through the Supabase backend. The backend retains a receipt hash and expiry, not the raw receipt. Oversized backups are rejected without replacing the saved snapshot.
